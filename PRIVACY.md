# ITC Guard — Privacy Policy

ITC Guard is a student project (VIT Pune) for reconciling GST input tax credit.

**What we receive:** files a trader uploads (purchase register, IMS, GSTR-2B), and
WhatsApp/email replies sent by suppliers to the demo business number.

**How we use it:** only to match invoices, track supplier follow-ups and summarise
supplier replies (using Google Gemini). We do not sell or share data with anyone else.

**Storage:** a MySQL database on AWS (Mumbai). Demo workspaces are deleted 30 days
after the last upload.

**Data deletion:** email architboraste2006@gmail.com with your phone number or
email address, and we will delete all messages and data linked to it within 7 days.
